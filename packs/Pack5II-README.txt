Church Synth - Preset Pack 5 II: Brass (Section engine)
The 20 sounds of Pack 5 rebuilt on the Section engine's Brass family: R&B and soul
brass, stabs, swells, the fall, sforzando-crescendo, staccato, a full blast,
trumpet / trombone / horn sections, French horns, solo, muted trumpet and flugelhorn.
Every preset is tagged and has "How it's built" notes. Names end in "II".
Needs Church Synth v0.6.0 or later (the Section engine).

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Pack 5 (the oscillator version) stays as
it was.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT
Each note is played by separate players. Dynamics makes brass much brighter as it
gets louder (each player's tone opens, the lip "pulse" narrows and a soft saturator
adds more): the bloom of real brass, instead of a synth filter sweep. Attacks:
Attack (soft to sforzando), Blat (a bright burst on loud notes) and Scoop (each note
starts below and rises into pitch). Bells: Trumpet, Trombone, Horn and Muted.

HOW TO PLAY THEM
  Velocity and the mod wheel both add Dynamics: play harder for brighter brass,
  push the wheel to swell a held chord (stabs and hits use velocity only).
  Solo Trumpet II / Muted Trumpet II / Flugelhorn II: legato slurs, aftertouch vibrato.
  Macros: 1 Brightness, 2 Space, 3 Vibrato, 4 a character control (Scoop, Blat, Length...).
