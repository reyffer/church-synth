Church Synth - Preset Pack 10: Twin Layer
20 presets in the style of the late-70s two-channel polyphonic flagship with
aftertouch (CS-80-style), tagged, each with "How it's built" notes.
Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 10 - Twin Layer") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Every preset carries the "Twin Layer" tag.

HOW THEY'RE BUILT
  every voice is TWO complete layers: Engine 1 -> Filter 1 and Engine 2 -> Filter 2
  (parallel), each with its own 12 dB filter and its own filter envelope (Env 2 / Env 3)
  TOUCH: velocity sets brightness; AFTERTOUCH (pressing into a held key) opens the
  filters and adds vibrato. No aftertouch on your keyboard? Macro 3 does the same.
  Ring modulation for bells and metallic tones; Ribbon Lead bends 7 semitones.

MACROS
  M1 Brightness    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 Growl / Touch / Pressure (= aftertouch) or a layer level
  M4 Attack / Decay / Release

WHAT'S IN IT
Brass: Noir Brass, Touch Brass, Brass Swell 80, Ring Mod Brass, Soft Horn Layer
Pads and strings: Twin Layer Strings, Noir Pad, Crystal Layer Pad, Breathing Strings,
  Cathedral Pad, Glass Harp
Keys and bells: Ring Mod Bells, Layer Electric Piano, Touch Clav
Leads: Ribbon Lead, Touch Lead, Soaring Sine Lead, Sub-Osc Trill Lead
Bass and arp: Twin Bass, Layer Arp
