Church Synth - Preset Pack 14: Special FX
20 sound-design effects (nature, sci-fi, transitions, oddities), tagged, each with
"How it's built" notes written as small sound-design lessons.
Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 14 - Special FX") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Every preset carries the "Special FX" tag.

HOW TO PLAY THEM
  Category FX (one-shots): press a key once (Thunder, Laser Zap, Sub Drop Impact...).
  Category Texture (beds): hold a key (Rain, Ocean Waves, Robot Chatter...).
  Clock Ticks, Computer Bleeps and Noise Sweep Up follow the host tempo.

TECHNIQUES COVERED
  filtered noise layers, pitch envelopes, sample & hold, quantised random pitch,
  ring modulation, one-shot Functions as long envelopes, curved envelopes,
  resonant bandpass whistles, sequencer and arpeggiator tricks (probability, random order)

WHAT'S IN IT
Nature: Thunder, Rain, Howling Wind, Ocean Waves, Campfire, Distant Storm
Sci-fi: Laser Zap, UFO Hover, Alarm Siren, Robot Chatter, Teleport, Computer Bleeps, Power Down
Transitions: Sub Drop Impact, Whoosh Pass, Reverse Crash, Noise Sweep Up
Oddities: Clock Ticks, Radio Tuning, Underwater Bubbles
