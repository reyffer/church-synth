Church Synth - Preset Pack 9: Vintage Poly
20 presets in the style of the late-70s five-voice classic polysynth (Prophet-style),
tagged, each with "How it's built" notes. Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 9 - Vintage Poly") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Every preset carries the "Vintage Poly" tag.

HOW THEY'RE BUILT
  two oscillators per voice (Osc 1 = the original's Osc B, Osc 2 = Osc A), saw and /
  or pulse, with analog drift + noise -> warm resonant 24 dB lowpass
  -> filter envelope + amp envelope
  POLY-MOD: the filter envelope and Osc B modulate Osc A (frequency, pulse width);
  with Osc A synced to Osc B that's the famous sync sweep
  WHEEL-MOD: the mod wheel brings in the LFO (PWM, vibrato or filter wobble)
  Unison stacks all voices on one note

MACROS
  M1 Brightness    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 a character control (Detune, Poly-Mod, Sweep, Clang, Spread...)
  M4 Attack / Decay / Release
The mod wheel does something useful on most presets (wheel-mod).

WHAT'S IN IT
Pads and strings: Vintage Poly Pad 5, Poly-Mod Pad, Analog Strings 5, Dark Sweep Pad,
  Hollow Pulse Pad, Wheel-Mod Strings
Brass: Vintage Brass 5, Soft Brass Pad, Poly-Mod Brass
Keys and bells: Vintage Pluck Keys, Poly-Mod Bells, Sync Sweep Keys, Synth Harpsichord, Glass Keys
Leads: Sync Sweep Lead, Unison Saw Lead, Wheel-Mod Lead
Bass and arp: Vintage Poly Bass, Unison Bass 5, Vintage Arp
