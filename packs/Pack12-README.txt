Church Synth - Preset Pack 12: EDM
20 plucks, stabs and leads for house, progressive, trance, future bass and big-room,
tagged, each with "How it's built" notes. Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 12 - EDM") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Every preset carries the "EDM" tag.

THE INGREDIENTS
  supersaw (7-voice Super unison), side-chain pumping (Function "Duck" on the amp,
  synced to the beat), pitch rises into notes and chords, chord stabs from one key
  (Chord unison), short plucks with big reverbs and dotted echoes.
Pumping sounds follow the host tempo.

MACROS
  M1 Brightness    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 a character control (Echo, Pump, Wobble, Resonance, Drive, Vowel...)
  M4 Decay / Release

WHAT'S IN IT
Plucks: Progressive Pluck, Tropical Pluck, Future Pluck, Trance Pluck, Bell Drop Pluck,
  Pumping Pluck, Dark Techno Pluck
Stabs: Big Room Stab, Future Bass Chords, House Organ Stab, Rave Stab, Piano House Stab,
  Pumping Saw Stab, Brass Drop Stab
Leads: Trance Supersaw Lead, Festival Lead, Future Bass Lead, Sync Screech Lead,
  Vocal Chop Lead, Drop Pluck Lead
