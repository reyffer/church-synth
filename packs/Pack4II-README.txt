Church Synth - Preset Pack 4 II: Orchestral Strings (Section engine)
The 20 sounds of Pack 4 rebuilt on the Section engine: full section, violins,
violas, cellos, basses, marcato, pizzicato, staccato, tremolo, flautando, an
ostinato, solo violin and cello, a chamber quartet and worship / cinematic beds.
Every preset is tagged and has "How it's built" notes. Names end in "II".
Needs Church Synth v0.6.0 or later (the Section engine).

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Pack 4 (the oscillator version) stays as
it was: keep both and compare.

WHAT IS DIFFERENT
In Pack 4 one oscillator with unison played the whole section. Here each note is
played by up to eight separate players, each with their own tuning, slow drift,
vibrato speed and depth, entry time and seat across the stereo field, and their sum
goes through a violin, viola, cello, bass or ensemble body. Vibrato moving the
harmonics through the body's fixed resonances is the shimmer of real strings.

HOW TO PLAY THEM
  Mod wheel = Dynamics on every preset (louder, brighter and more vibrato together),
  the orchestral-library convention. At rest most presets play mezzo-forte; "Full
  Strings Dynamic II" starts at pianissimo.
  Solo Violin II / Solo Cello II: play legato for slurs; aftertouch adds vibrato.
  Macros: 1 Brightness, 2 Space, 3 Vibrato, 4 Attack (or Length / Bite / Air).

AN HONEST NOTE
Still synthesis, not a sampled orchestra: closer than Pack 4, light on CPU (about
half of an analog pad for eight notes of six players), and expressive under the
mod wheel.
