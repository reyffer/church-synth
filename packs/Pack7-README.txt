Church Synth - Preset Pack 7: Grand Poly
20 presets in the style of the 80s 8-voice flagship polysynth (Jupiter-style), tagged,
each with "How it's built" notes. Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 7 - Grand Poly") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Every preset carries the "Grand Poly" tag;
a rename you made to that tag is applied to these too.

HOW THEY'RE BUILT
  TWO oscillators per voice, detuned and drifting (the width comes from them, not a chorus)
  cross-mod (VCO 2 modulating VCO 1) and hard sync (VCO 2 locked to VCO 1)
  -> non-resonant highpass -> 4-pole lowpass, switchable to 2-pole (OTA model)
  TWO envelopes: one for the filter (and pitch / pulse width), one for the volume
  Unison (all voices stacked on one note) and Dual (two layered patches)
Compare with Pack 6 (Chorus Poly): one oscillator plus a chorus versus two
oscillators and no chorus. Same era, very different approach.

MACROS
  M1 Brightness    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 a character control (Detune, Swell, Spread, Metal, Sync Depth...)
  M4 Attack / Decay / Release

WHAT'S IN IT
Pads and strings: Grand Strings, Grand Warm Pad, Envelope PWM Pad,
  Dual Strings and Bells, Resonant Sweep Strings, Glass Pulse Pad
Brass: Grand Brass, Brass Swell Stack, Unison Brass Stab, Dual Brass and Strings
Keys and bells: Cross-Mod Bells, Sync Keys, Bright Poly Pluck, Synth Piano 80s, Grand Arpeggio
Leads: Unison Lead, Sync Lead, Soft Square Lead
Bass: Unison Bass, Sync Bass
