Church Synth - Preset Pack 2: Worship Elements
50 presets, tagged, each with "How it's built" notes (shown under the browser).
Needs Church Synth v0.2 or later (v0.2.1 recommended: it smooths velocity changes on
legato notes, which the sub basses use).

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 2 - Worship Elements") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. The sounds are in the User bank, in their
categories (Pad, Texture, FX, Pluck, Keys, Sequence, Bass, Strings/Brass) and under
their tags. Every preset has the "Worship" tag.

TAGS (rename or delete them in the browser: right-click a tag)
  Pad, Drone, Texture, Transition, Riser, Swell, Evolving, Shimmer, Choir, Strings,
  Bells, Mallet, Piano, Felt, Pluck, Delay, Synth Keys, Arp, Pulsing, Bass, Sub,
  Fifths, Warm, Dark, Bright, Big, Lo-Fi, Ambient, Cinematic, Polysynth, Organ,
  Tonewheel, Worship

MACROS (same idea on every preset)
  M1 Brightness / Tone    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 a character control (Motion, Vowel, Shimmer, Pulse, Sparkle...)
  M4 Swell / Attack / Decay / Length
"Motion" on the pads adds slow movement: at zero the pad is steady.

TEMPO
Pulsing pads, drones, arps, the Heartbeat Sub, the risers and the echoes follow the
host tempo (MainStage, Logic...). Risers start when you press the key: press on the
downbeat, two or four bars before the drop.

LEVELS
Pads, keys and bells are level-matched with the factory set (about -4 dBFS peak for
a four-note chord). Drones and textures (category Texture) are matched for ONE held
note at about -7 dBFS, because that's how they're played. Riser and sweep levels
are matched at their loudest point.

WHAT'S IN IT
Foundation pads: Sanctuary Pad, Warm Blanket, Morning Light, Open Fifths, Octave Air,

All sounds are synthesised by Church Synth itself: no samples.
