Church Synth - Preset Pack 15: Acoustic Pianos
20 pianos built entirely from synthesis (grands, soul and gospel, uprights, character
pianos and layers), tagged, each with "How it's built" notes written as small
sound-design lessons.
Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 15 - Acoustic Pianos") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. Every preset carries the "Piano" tag.

AN HONEST NOTE
These are synthesised pianos, not samples. They won't fool anyone next to a sampled
concert grand in a quiet room, and they aren't meant to replace your main piano
patch. They show how close synthesis gets with a few well-chosen ingredients, they
are light on CPU, and in a band mix several of them do the job very well.

HOW TO PLAY THEM
  Velocity matters: soft playing is quieter AND darker, hard playing is brighter.
  There is no sustain on the amp envelope: notes always die away, faster up high.
  Use your sustain pedal as on a real piano.
  Macros on every grand: 1 Tone, 2 Space, 3 a character control, 4 Decay.

TECHNIQUES COVERED
  unison voices started in phase (piano strings), "double decay" with a fast-fading
  strike layer, velocity to brightness / wavetable position / level, key-tracked
  decay, exponential decay curves, hammer noise, detuning as character (honky-tonk),
  tape wow with an LFO, macros as route sidechains, FM for struck metal (toy piano),
  layering with a delayed swell (piano + pad / strings)

WHAT'S IN IT
Grands: Concert Grand, Studio Grand, Warm Grand, Bright Pop Grand, Soft Grand,
        Ballad Grand, Rock Grand, Worship Grand
Soul / R&B / Gospel: Soul Ballad Piano, Neo-Soul Piano, Gospel Grand
Uprights: Upright Piano, Honky-Tonk, Old Upright
Character: Muted Piano, Lo-Fi Piano, Dark Cinematic Piano, Toy Piano
Layers: Piano and Pad, Piano and Strings
