Church Synth - Preset Pack 3: Electric Pianos
30 presets, tagged, each with "How it's built" notes (shown under the browser).
Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 3 - Electric Pianos") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser. The sounds are in the User bank, category
Keys, and under their tags.

THE FOUR FAMILIES (all synthesised, no samples)
  Tine I   tine-and-pickup piano, the earlier kind (Rhodes-style): more bark and growl
  Tine II  the later kind: cleaner, more bell on top
  FM       two-operator FM piano (DX-style): glassy tine, soft body
  Reed     reed electric piano (Wurlitzer-style): nasal, raspy when played hard
  Clav     plucked-string keyboard (Clavinet-style): thin, funky, percussive

These are synth-built electric pianos. They respond to velocity like the real ones
(soft = round, hard = bark / rasp), but they aren't sampled instruments.

TAGS (rename or delete them in the browser: right-click a tag)
  Electric Piano, Tine, FM, Reed, Clav, Funk, Tremolo, Chorus, Phaser, Driven,
  Lo-Fi, Bells, Pad, Worship

MACROS (same idea on every preset)
  M1 Bark (tine and FM) / Brightness (reed and clav)    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 a character control (Grit, Pan, Chorus, Drive, Phase, Tremolo, Bell, Wah...)
  M4 Decay
High notes die away sooner than low ones, like a real electric piano.

WHAT'S IN IT
Tine I: Tine I Stage, Tine I Suitcase, Tine I Ballad, Tine I Growl, Tine Lo-Fi
Tine II: Tine II Stage, Tine II Phaser, Tine II Chorus, Tine II Bell
FM: FM Ballad EP, FM Glass EP, FM Soft EP, FM Bright EP, FM Bell EP, FM Wide EP,
  FM Keys and Pad, FM Punch EP, FM Velvet EP, FM Soul EP, FM Hollow EP
Reed: Reed Classic, Reed Tremolo, Reed Driven, Reed Soft, Reed Phaser
Clav: Clav Funk, Clav Muted, Clav Wah, Clav Phaser, Clav Mellow

All presets are level-matched with the factory set (about -4 dBFS peak for a
four-note chord). The clavs are percussive, so at the same peak they sound a little
quieter than the pianos.
