Church Synth - Preset Pack 4: Orchestral Strings
20 presets, tagged, each with "How it's built" notes (shown under the browser).
Needs Church Synth v0.2.1 or later.

INSTALL
Copy this whole folder ("Pack 4 - Orchestral Strings") into:
  Mac:     Documents/Church Synth/Presets
  Windows: Documents\Church Synth\Presets
Then press Rescan in the preset browser.

ABOUT THESE SOUNDS
Synth strings with an orchestral approach: section sizes, articulations, body
resonance, bow noise and vibrato. They blend beautifully with a band, but they
aren't a sampled orchestra and won't pass for one when exposed.

MACROS
  M1 Brightness    M2 Space (reverb send)
  M3 Vibrato (or another character control)    M4 Attack (Length on short notes)
Harder playing gives a faster bow on the sustained sounds.
Full Strings Dynamic uses the mod wheel for dynamics (down = soft and dark,
up = loud and bright), like orchestral sample libraries.
Solo Violin and Solo Cello: aftertouch adds vibrato.
